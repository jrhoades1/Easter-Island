# Mamari (tablet C) images used for the transcription audit

The calendar passage Ca6–Ca9 is the measurement this audit checks. Mamari is the tablet that carries it. An openly licensed photograph of the original wood exists. Higher-resolution photographs of Small Santiago and Great Santiago that turned up in the same search are either reproductions or were not needed once Mamari was readable enough to bound the calendar and not readable enough to renumber it.
