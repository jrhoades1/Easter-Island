The Rongorongo of Easter Island

Metoro's Reading of Tablet Aruku-Kurenga: Br10

Br10.    [Show hieroglyphs]     (X)
=01= ka mai ki te Raa __ kua vaha __ ko te henua __ kua puu ko te henua __
=05= kua hakaora ia raua __ kua hora koia __ kua hakatetea ko raua __ ki to ihe __ kua rere ki ruga __
=10= [p.118] o te marama __ ko te ika __ kua hagai __ ma to koe tagata era ra ko to ihe __ kua rere ki to ragi __
=15= kua moe ko hau tea __ ki ruga o te tagata __ kikiu atu __ kua rere te manu __ i te tagata ure huki __
=20= no ona __ koia anake __ ka mou ia __ mai tae tuki te henua __ kua moe ko te manu __
=25= ki to ika __ e tagata haga era ki te mea ke __ ko agaagata __ kua maa __ ko koe __
=30= e kua hatu ko to toa __ e tagata haga __ i to ua e kua koti ko te henua __ ma te maitaki __ koia ra kua hoki to rarahoi __
=35= kua tu ki to toga __ E tagata itiiti
Tournez au verso de la tablette à la plus haute ligne de 1'échancrure, faisant suite à celle que vous venez de lire au recto. [Turn over to the verso of the tablet for the topmost line at the notch, continuing the line you have just read on the recto]
