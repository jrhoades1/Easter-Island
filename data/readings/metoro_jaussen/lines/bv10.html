The Rongorongo of Easter Island

Metoro's Reading of Tablet Aruku-Kurenga: Bv10

Bv10.    [Show hieroglyphs]     (XX)
=01= Ma te kava ra __ e kava haati ia __ kua moe ma te tara huki __ kua ati ra te kava __
=05= kua hakaroa __ ko te kava ma te hatu huri __ i vai o amo hia __ eko te maitaki ma te hokohuki ku hura hia __ ki te vai o amo hia __
=10= ko te ravarava ku moe __ eaha te goe moe __ ki te henua __ e tagata era e __ e ka piripiri raua __
=15= mai tae heu hia __ ma te inoino ma te hoi hatu __ ihe hokohuki ka rere te manu __ i te inoino a to hato __ i te hoko huki ka tara ia __
=20= ma te hokohuki __ ka inoino ma hatu huri __ ka hahaú hia __ ma te maitaki __ koia kua oho __
=25= kua rave i to henua te moa gutu-pikopiko __ ka oho ka rere [p.146] ki te manu vaero rua ma te hokohuki __ ki te ika __ ka hoko ka rave __ ta au o te ika __
=30= o te hau tea __ ma to ua o te inoino ma te hatu huri __ e nuku noho vai __ ko te marama __ kua katea i ruga __
=35= koia ra ko te hiro ma te nuku __ kua tau te ragi i ruga __ e Rei vage huki __ e tagata tuu ra i te toga __ ihe amo hia __
=40= ka tuu i to rima __ ka moe ka hakareva __ mai tae oho ki to toga __ kua moe koe __ ki to toga nui __
=45= mai tae e hakaruoi __
