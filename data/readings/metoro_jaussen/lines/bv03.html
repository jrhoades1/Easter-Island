The Rongorongo of Easter Island

Metoro's Reading of Tablet Aruku-Kurenga: Bv3

Bv3.    [Show hieroglyphs]     (XIII)
=01= ko te nuku __ ku tara huki __ kua hama te nuku __ mai tae te tagata __
=05= hua ra __ ki te henua __ koia __ kua hoki to maro __ e manu rere ra __
=10= ki te inoino __ ka rere te manu __ haati kava __ mai tae tuki to tauuru __ nuku __
=15= te uru papageti __ tagata oho __ ki te huaga __ tagata oho __ ki to toa __
=20= ko te marama kua haga i ruga ia ia __ ko te toa __ [p.126] E tagata hakatu ki to ika e vaha te ika __ ma te ua __ kua pua te hahine __
=25= kua tere ko to haga __ ki te tagata haga __ ki tona mea ke __ kua oho koia ki te haga __ mai moe koia ki te mago __
=30= kua haga i tona mea ke __ ko te marama kua haga i tona mea ke __ ma te hakaua __ koia kua haga i to kava __ kua haati ia e kava __
=35= ko te kava o Motoipua __ ko te marama kua haga i tona mea ke __ o te kava kua haati ke __ mai tae tu __ ki te hipu __
=40= gutu huri __ e toa maro __ kua vero hia raua __ ki to maro __ ma to kava __
=45= kua huri te hatu ki te henua __ ma te maitaki __ mai tae vere hia __ ki te pito o te henua __ e nuku hoi __
=50= kua here te toa __ i ruga o to maro __
