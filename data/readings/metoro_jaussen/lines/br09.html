The Rongorongo of Easter Island

Metoro's Reading of Tablet Aruku-Kurenga: Br9

Br9.    [Show hieroglyphs]     (IX)
=01= ki te ahi __ kua rere ki te ragi __ kua hati ïa i to toa __ i rapue __
=05= ko te tagata kua haga __ ki te mea ke __ ku hakatega koia __ ki mua o te tagata __ hua ia __
=10= e tara tuu __ o to vaha __ o te marama __ ko te marama __ kua haga ia i te ika __
=15= e kua rere ko te manu __ ki te huaaga __ o to kava __ kua huri ko to hatu __ kua tupu ko to toa __
=20= koia ra ki te nuku __ e kua tupu i te tauuru __ e tagata ui ra __ ki te huaga __ kua rere ko te manu __
=25= ki to tea ko to toa __ kua haga ki te marama __ [p.116] kua tupu te toa __ ko te manu __ kua haga ia ki te toa __
=30= te kava ku huri ïa __ ki to hatu __ e tagata era e kai __ i te toa e __ tagata kai __
=35= i te vaha __ koia ra kua kai i tona mea __ e kua rave i to haga __ e tagata e __ rave ra __
=40= ki te nuku kai __ o to ua __ to inoino o to hure __ koia ra __ kua haga ki te mea ke __
=45= e tagata moe ra __ ki mua e __ ki te verega o to kava __ kua hati hia mai ko to kava __ e kua haga hia i to topa __
=50= koia ra kua hakavari __ ki te henua __ kua hua hia mai __ ki te henua ko te henua kua noho __ ki to ariki __
=55= ko to ariki kua mau ki tona tuura __ e manu itiiti era e __
