The Rongorongo of Easter Island

Metoro's Reading of Tablet Tahua: Aa5

Aa5.    [Show hieroglyphs]     (XIII).
=01= Erua agaagata __ i te henua __ i te hau tea __ kua hura te tagata __
=05= tona hura i roto i te pa __ eaha te huri __ o te manu kukurutou __ ko te manu eve pepepepe __ ko te maitaki __
=10= ihe aki pu __ ko te maitaki __ ka kau te nahe __ ko te tagata kua moe __ ki to manu ko te tagata kai __
=15= ki to moa __ ko te tagata kua mau __ ki te hau tea __ ko te kaiga o te vari __ kua ihi te vae __
=20= ki to ragi __ e gana Rei __ [p.210] e kua hagana ia __ ki to Rei __ mo to moa __
=25= ko te tagata kua ui __ kia ia __ kua rere ko te manu __ e te tagata vae ihi __ e kua tupu te pua __
=30= i te henua __ mai tae tuu __ ki te henua vae rorua __ i te henua __ ki to vae rua __
=35= kua tupu te kaho __ i te henua __ ka piripiri __ i te henua __ te maitaki __
=40= e ka hakatupu __ ka pu i te niu __ ka pu i te poporo __ ka pu i te toromiro __ o te hetu __
=45= no te henua __ te hokohuki __ maitaki __ te tagata __ i te henua __
=50= ka hahaúa __ ko te tagata ka kai tona mea __ ma te nuku rua __ mai hiti rua a neva __ ma te ruaki ki te kena __
=55= i mau ki te maro __ ma te kupega o maro __ ki te kupega o maro __ e henua ko to ihe __ e tagata kua here tona mea i ruga __
=60= kua moe kua oho __ ki to ihe __ ka puhi ki te ahi __ e ahi __ mo to vahi __
=65= kua hiri te rima o agaagata __ ki te hau tea __ [p.212] ma to ihe __ i te ahi - ko to ahi __ mo to ahi __
=70= e ika moe i te henua __ ko te tagata __ ka he i to pure __ kua boko ia __ ki roto to pure __
=75= kua here ia kua oho __ ki to hakarava __ kua here i te vae __ o te ariki __ o te hetu __
=80= kua rere ko te hetu __ ki to manu __ ku vovou mai __ a ia hanau mai __ o mauga tuu tara __
=85= e kua moe te goe e hiko __ ko te mauga __ mai tae tu ki te nuku rua __ o to ragi __ ko te tagata ui __
=90= ki tona henua erua __ e manu pao __ i tona ihe.
