The Rongorongo of Easter Island

Metoro's Reading of Tablet Tahua: Aa7

Aa7.    [Show hieroglyphs]     (XV).
=01= Kua oho kia ia __ ki te mauga __ ki te tapamea __ kua pure ia __
=05= ihe tao mau __ ma to ragi __ kua mau ia i te nuku i te rima __ moa kai hia ki te tagata __ ki te maitaki oona __
=10= tagata __ [p.218] mau i te heheu ana __ o te maitaki __ o te maharoga __ kua noho ia __
=15= kua rere ki to manu __ ki to Rei __ e ariki ia __ kua noho ki te henua __ ki te ragi __
=20= ma te tagata vae ihi __ kua tui i tana ika __ i te maitaki __ i te maharoga __ ka pipiri te hetu __
=25= ka tau avaga __ ko te marama __ ka noho i te ragi __ e tagata nuku tui __ i te maro __
=30= ko te tagata __ tui i te ika __ i te maitaki __ i te maharoga __ pipiri te hetu __
=35= tau avaga __ rere te tagata ui __ i te marama __ i to ragi __ ko te ika vaha ihi __
=40= u hanau ia __ mai tae tuu ki te maka nuku __ kua oho te tagata __ tui ika __ te maitaki __
=45= te maharoga __ ka pipiri te hetu __ te ihe tau __ ki to honu __ e tagata poopouo __
=50= ma te kona heke __ e tapamea __ ihe purega __ ki te henua __ ki te ragi __
=55= ka piri te ragi ura te ragi kaa __ ku hanau ki tona ika __ i te maitaki __ i te maharoga __ ka pipiri te hetu __
=60= ma to ihe __ ko te ariki moa __ [p.220] mo te hakagana __ kia ia __ i tuu ia ki te purega haga __
=65= tapamea __ ki te henua __ ki te ragi __ o te tagata hanau __ kua oho te vae o te tagata __
=70= tui ika __ e maitaki __ e pipiri te hetu __ ihe ihe tau __ ka kau te honu __
=75= e tagata ia kua kai __ i to heke __ e tapamea __ e purega __ i te henua __
=80= i te ragi __ kua hanau ia __ ki toona herehua __ maitaki __ pipiri te hetu __
=85= ihe ihe tau __ e nuku __ ma pure __ i te henua __ i te ragi __
=90= tagata __ mau tao hia __ ihe ihe tau __ ma te henua __ maitaki __
=95= to heu kua iri te rima __ ko te henua __ kua oho te rima __ ma to ihe __ ko te henna __
=100= maitaki __ kua hua te hue __ i to maro __ te hau tea __ ma te hoko kua huki ia __
=105= kua oho ia __ ki te tao mau __ eko to ihe __ ki to henua __ maitaki __
=110= o to maro __ kua haú __ ki te ariki.
