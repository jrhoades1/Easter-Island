# Native readings (Track 4)

Texts used to test whether a recorded Rapa Nui "reading" lines up with the Barthel coding of a tablet. No glyph is given a meaning in these files.

| Directory | What it is | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `thomson_1891/` | William J. Thomson, *Te Pito te Henua, or Easter Island* (Report of the U.S. National Museum for the year ending June 30, 1889; Washington: Government Printing Office, 1891). Ure Vaeiko's chants, as printed. | Public domain. HTML snapshots are the Kohaumotu transcription of those pages. |
| `metoro_jaussen/` | Metoro Tauʻa Ure's sign-by-sign words, written down by Bishop Florentin Étienne (Tepano) Jaussen in the 1870s. | Jaussen died in 1891; the notebook wording is public domain. The HTML is Kohaumotu's transcription, copied under that site's notice (acknowledge the source; non-profit). |

Barthel 1958 and Fischer 1997 are not copied here. Sign numbers come from the Barthel pages already vendored under `tests/fixtures/`.

The alignment code is `parsers/native_readings.py`. The write-up is `docs/decipherment/track4_native_readings.md`.
