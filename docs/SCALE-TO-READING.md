## Scoreboard vocabulary

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **HOLD** | Claim true: zero leak **and** complete set vs the locked population. |
| **incomplete-set LOSE** | Existing n-grams may be I-only / zero leak, but some sites lack the window (line edge). Record it; do not “fix” by shrinking *n* mid-cycle. |
| **leak LOSE** | Any off-I / off-population hit. Stops that peel. |
| **WIN** | Reserved. Do not declare a reading from a HOLD. |

## What not to scale

- End-to-end “translate the tablet” (LLM gloss of a whole line/side).
- Bigger models, new providers, or prompt stacks **without** a fail test.
- **On-demand spend.** Included Models only. If included is exhausted, **stop**.
- Detector retune, new tablet scrape, invented Barthel, or Ib/Ir pages that are not published.
- Re-locking a closed peel (leftover-5, leftover-6 extra-I, previous *n* already LOSE) as if it were new work.
- Parallel “creative” cycles that skip the one-hypothesis / one-scoreboard rule.
